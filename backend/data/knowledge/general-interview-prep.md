# General Interview Preparation

## Before the interview
- Research the company: product, recent news, mission, competitors.
- Review the job description and map your experience to each requirement.
- Prepare 3-5 STAR stories in advance that can flex across multiple question
  types (a project, a conflict, a failure, a leadership moment, a
  cross-team collaboration).
- Prepare 2-3 thoughtful questions to ask the interviewer — about the team,
  the roadmap, or how success is measured in the role. Avoid questions
  answered by the company's public website.

## During the interview
- Arrive (or log on) 5-10 minutes early.
- Maintain a conversational tone — an interview is a two-way evaluation, not
  an interrogation.
- It's OK to pause and think before answering; a brief silence beats a
  rambling non-answer.
- Keep behavioral answers to roughly 60-90 seconds; if the interviewer wants
  more detail, they'll ask a follow-up.

## Answering "tell me about yourself"
Structure: current role → most relevant past experience → why you're
interested in this role/company. Keep it under 2 minutes and tailor it to
the job, not a full career narration.

## Red flags interviewers watch for
- Blaming others for failures with no self-reflection.
- Vague answers with no specifics or numbers.
- Not being able to explain your own past decisions.
- Badmouthing a previous employer.

## After the interview
- Send a brief thank-you note within 24 hours referencing something specific
  from the conversation.
- Reflect on which questions felt weak and prepare better stories for next
  time.
