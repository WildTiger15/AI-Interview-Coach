# Common Behavioral Interview Questions

These questions test soft skills, judgment, and self-awareness rather than
technical knowledge. All of them are best answered with the STAR method.

## Teamwork & conflict
- Tell me about a time you disagreed with a teammate. How did you resolve it?
- Describe a situation where you had to work with someone difficult.
- Tell me about a time you had to give someone critical feedback.

Good answers focus on the *process* used to resolve disagreement (listening,
finding shared goals, compromising on approach) rather than "I was right and
they came around."

## Failure & learning
- Tell me about a time you failed. What did you learn?
- Describe a mistake you made and how you fixed it.

Interviewers are checking for accountability. Avoid answers that blame
others or pick a "failure" that's secretly a humblebrag (e.g. "I worked too
hard"). Own the mistake, show the concrete fix, show what changed afterward.

## Leadership & ownership
- Tell me about a time you took initiative without being asked.
- Describe a project you led. What was the outcome?
- Tell me about a time you had to make a decision with incomplete information.

## Prioritization & pressure
- Tell me about a time you had too much to do and not enough time.
- Describe how you handled a tight deadline.

Good answers explain the *criteria* used to prioritize (impact, urgency,
dependencies) not just "I worked overtime."

## Motivation & fit
- Why do you want to work here?
- Where do you see yourself in five years?
- What's a professional accomplishment you're proud of?

For "why this company," specifics beat flattery — reference the product,
team, or mission, not generic praise.
